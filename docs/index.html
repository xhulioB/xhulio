MVXB Design & Build
MVXB is the design studio of Marissa Volk and Xhulio Binjaku. We are architectural designers, urban strategists, and builders based in Philadelphia.

Marissa Volk is an architectural and urban designer specializing in placemaking, community engagement, and graphics. She has experience in urban design and placemaking and urban development consulting. She has a MA in Graphic Design and BA in Architecture from the University of Florida.

Xhulio Binjaku is a designer, academic, and licensed general contractor. He has experience in community development and urban planning for the City of Philadelphia. He has taught architecture studios and seminars at the University of Pennsylvania, Princeton, and MIT. He has a MArch from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, a MA in Architecture History & Theory from Princeton University, and two BA's (one in architecture one in geography) from the University of Florida. He is an PhD Candidate in Architecture History & Theory at Princeton.
