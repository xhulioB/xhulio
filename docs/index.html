Xhulio Binjaku is a designer, architectural historian, and urban planner. He worked in community development & urban planning for the City of Philadelphia. He has a MArch from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, a MA in Architecture History & Theory from Princeton University, & two BA's (one in architecture & one in geography) from the University of Florida. He is a PhD Candidate in Architecture History & Theory at Princeton. He teaches architecture courses at University of Pennsylvania.
